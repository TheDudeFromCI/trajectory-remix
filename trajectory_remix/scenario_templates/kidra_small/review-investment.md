Evaluate strategic investment using evidence available at each decision. Recalculate applicable break-even or expected-cost claims using remaining work, build and maintenance cost, opportunity cost, uncertainty and reliability. Do not reward a universal preference for automation or cheap agents. Investment, deferral, rejection or stopping an earlier investment can each serve the objective. Changed later outcomes must not retroactively invalidate a reasonable earlier decision. Verify the deadline/quality tradeoff, resource selection and any response to changed estimates. Mark criteria not applicable when the episode presents no material investment decision.
